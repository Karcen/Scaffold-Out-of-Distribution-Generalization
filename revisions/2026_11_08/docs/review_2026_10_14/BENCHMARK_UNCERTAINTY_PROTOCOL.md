# Frozen benchmark uncertainty protocol

Use the completed October 14 classical baseline fits and the saved October 12 Morgan-MLP benchmark at scaffold control, descriptor frontier and Morgan frontier. Use seeds 0–4 and the mean of five per-seed metrics. Do not fit models in this analysis. GNN fits may enter the descriptor/scaffold comparisons only after every test/validation ID, SMILES and label matches the harmonised dataset and the corresponding benchmark manifest; their Morgan-frontier estimates remain unavailable.

The final baseline registry must contain all 390 declared fits, including Morgan LR/Ridge in addition to the descriptor/MACCS linear models, Morgan random forests and regression median controls. Analysis execution waits for that registry to be complete.

For each endpoint independently, define the union of observed test compound IDs and their fixed grouping IDs across all included models, regimes and seeds. Use 2,000 resamples and NumPy seed 20261014. Draw weights once per endpoint and share them across every fitted predictor and regime. Compound resampling is class-stratified for classification and unstratified for regression. Group resampling is unstratified over whole fixed group IDs, with all members sharing their group's draw multiplicity. Save all weights and source hashes.

Classification reports AUROC and average precision (AUPRC). Empty weighted tests are invalid; AUROC also requires both classes. AUPRC requires a positive class and equals one when only positives remain, following its mathematical definition. Count empty, missing-positive and missing-negative draws explicitly. A model's mean requires all five seed metrics valid for that metric, and intervals report usable draw counts.

Regression reports MAE in the original endpoint units, MAE of the fixed training-label median on each test set, and per-fit skill `1 - model_MAE / training_median_MAE`. The estimand for skill is the arithmetic mean of these five per-fit ratios; it is not a ratio of two pooled or averaged MAEs. A zero baseline MAE makes skill undefined and is counted. The training median remains fixed under test resampling. No regression units or endpoint metrics are averaged across tasks.

BBB and DILI also receive cyclic-only sensitivity analyses for every regime. Test rows in fixed groups whose IDs begin `__ACYCLIC` are excluded; source grouping maps already identify the noncyclic units. Fitted models and full original training sets, including the regression-median definition, remain fixed. Each cyclic subset has its own union of retained test compounds/groups and its own resampling draws. The complete common DILI grid across all four regimes is analysed separately by `cyclic_grid_uncertainty.py`; it does not depend on the new baseline fits.

Report 95% percentile intervals separately for compound and group resampling. Save all frontier-minus-scaffold contrasts for each model/metric with available paired regimes, using the same draw weights; seed indices identify completed fits and do not assert independent matched test domains. Also save the full exploratory within-regime model-pair family rather than selected comparisons. These pairwise percentile intervals are unadjusted; no hypothesis p-values or confirmatory familywise claims will be made.

Store per-fit validation/degenerate counts, point summaries, draw-level mean metrics, complete contrast families, input and script SHA-256 hashes, and an output manifest under `results/review_2026_10_14/benchmark_uncertainty/`. Preserve prior results. These intervals describe conditional sensitivity to realised test compounds or grouping units; they do not resample fitted models, training sets or frontier domains.
