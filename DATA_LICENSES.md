## CMNPD

- Local source name: CMNPD_1.0_calc_prop.csv
- Source: <https://docs.cmnpd.org/downloads>
- Terms: <https://docs.cmnpd.org/terms-and-conditions>
- ADMET field definitions:
  <https://docs.cmnpd.org/frequently-asked-questions/parameter-questions>
- Database paper: Lyu et al., *Nucleic Acids Research* 49(D1), D509-D515 (2021),
  DOI 10.1093/nar/gkaa763
- Stated licence: CC BY-NC-SA 4.0
- Recorded SHA-256:
  cdccc99060070c9ff8f56b40a206e79c05ef3c5d6c99fe3eb759422fc8a75c23

The official download page currently names its calculated-properties export with
a `.tsv` suffix, whereas the supplied local analysis input is a comma-separated
`.csv`.  The hash above identifies the exact local input; we do not claim that it
is byte-identical to the site's current export.  Reproductions using a freshly
downloaded TSV should preserve the documented column names and record a new hash.

CMNPD's six ADMET fields used here are computational Pipeline Pilot annotations,
not experimental outcomes. The raw CMNPD CSV is intentionally excluded from the
anonymous supplementary archive. Researchers must obtain it from the official
site and comply with its attribution, non-commercial and share-alike conditions.
Derived teacher-agreement summaries are not a relicensing of the source database.
